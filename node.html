Naga Node · In build · Your choice

Your laptop takes a night shift.

Naga Node is what $NAGA is for. It runs on peaqOS, and a laptop with $NAGA bonded to it can join.

A laptop sits idle most of the day. With Naga Node on, yours rents out the internet and computing power you aren't using, and partner networks pay it for the work. We'll ask you at checkout. Say no and nothing changes.

Where things stand in November 2026: $NAGA is live on Machine.Fun. We are still building Naga Node, bonded laptops, staking and the discount. We'll date each one on this page as it opens.

  • Network pay

    Partner networks pay your laptop for the bandwidth it carries and the computing it does.

  • Machine Rewards

    We put 30% of our $NAGA creator fees into a pool. Each month we pay it out to the bonded laptops that worked, by how much each one did. We pay in USDC, a dollar stablecoin. A laptop collects at most twice its network pay for that month. The rest goes to our treasury.

Before you say yes

  • It's pay for work

    Your laptop earns for what it does. Holding $NAGA earns nothing.

  • These are estimates

    We expect about ₱1,000 a month from a SIPAG I and ₱1,500–1,800 from a LAKAN II. At that rate a SIPAG I covers its own price in about four and a half years, after power. We guarantee no amount, so buy it because you want the laptop.

  • GPU work rarely pays here

    A LAKAN II renting out its RTX 5060 at full load grosses about US$1.40 a day at today's public GPU-rental rates. In Manila it burns about US$1.05 of power doing it. We switch GPU jobs on only where your power is cheap enough.

  • Your power, your internet

    Strangers' traffic travels through your connection. Use unlimited home internet and read your ISP's terms. Prepaid data will burn through its load.

  • The drive holds the keys

    Your laptop's wallet lives on its drive. Wipe, reinstall or swap that drive and you lose the bond link and any rewards you haven't claimed. We can't restore them. Claim first, and write down your recovery phrase.

  • Rewards arrive as USDC

    USDC is a dollar stablecoin on Solana, and it lands in your laptop's wallet. Our fees reach us in $MU, a token that tracks Micron Technology's share price, so the pool we convert each month grows and shrinks with that stock. Some countries restrict stablecoins. Where they do, a laptop can't collect Machine Rewards.

  • You handle the tax

    Your government may count rewards as income. You report them.

  • You can quit

    Turn Naga Node off whenever you like. The laptop runs the same without it.

Where rewards come from

Trading fees fund the rewards.

$NAGA trades on Machine.Fun, on Raydium (Solana), against $MU, a token that tracks Micron Technology's share price. We held no presale and we sell no $NAGA. We collect 1.5% of each trade as a creator fee, and it reaches us in $MU: on a trade worth US$100, that is US$1.50. Micron has no part in $NAGA or Naga Node. We split the fees three ways, into wallets you can audit.
