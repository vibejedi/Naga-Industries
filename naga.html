$NAGA Bonded machines

Every machine carries a bond.

Naga Node · Opt-in

Idle time, put to work.

Every bonded machine ships with peaqOS. If you switch on Naga Node when you buy, your machine can share spare internet, storage or computing power with partner networks while you are not using it, and it is paid for the work it does. It is off by default, and you can switch it off at any time.

  • Network rewards

    Partner networks pay your machine directly for the bandwidth, storage or computing it supplies.

  • Machine Rewards

    Half of the creator fees Naga receives from $NAGA trading go into a pool. Each month it is shared among active bonded machines, according to the work each one did.

Before you opt in

  • Rewards are pay for work

    They are payment for what your machine does. They are not interest, dividends or a return on $NAGA.

  • Nothing is guaranteed

    Rewards change month to month. Naga does not promise any amount, and a machine will not necessarily pay for itself.

  • It uses your power and internet

    Partner traffic passes through your connection. Use unlimited home internet, not prepaid data, and check that your plan allows it.

  • Your drive holds the keys

    The machine's wallet and peaqOS identity live on its drive. If you wipe, reinstall or replace the drive, the bond link and any unclaimed rewards are lost, and Naga cannot restore them. Claim your rewards and back up your recovery phrase first.

  • Taxes are yours

    Rewards may count as taxable income where you live. Reporting them is your responsibility.

  • You stay in control

    You choose what to share, and you can leave Naga Node whenever you like. Your machine works the same without it.

What $NAGA is not

Not required
