## What changed on 13 November 2026, and why

- **Status moved to post 2.** The old thread asked "What can it earn?" in the present tense while nothing ran.
  A reader now learns that no laptop earns yet before reading any figure.
- **The bond is described as what ships with the laptop.** "We buy $NAGA on the open market" is gone from the
  thread. It is true, and the page still says it, but in a post it reads as a buyback, which is the company's
  effort holding up the token.
- **No trading venue and no "1.5% of each trade".** Naming where $NAGA trades points buyers to it, and tying
  rewards to trading volume makes them read as a share of trading. The post says "creator fees"; the page
  carries the detail.
- **The backpack for the first 300 stakers is out of the thread.** A "first 300" offer gives people a reason to
  buy and stake quickly, and it needs a DTI sales promotion permit. Add it back once the permit is in hand.
- **"What $NAGA is not" has its own post**, with "holding or staking it pays nothing" and "we don't sell it".
- **peaqOS.** The thread says Naga Node "runs on peaqOS" and that "the networks that use them" pay the laptop.
  It no longer says partner networks "on peaqOS" pay, because peaq has not confirmed that peaqOS supplies the
  paid work (its public docs describe identity, bonding and a work record).
- **No emoji.**
