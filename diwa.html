Meet Baku

Baku

Our own AI, running entirely on DIWA. It speaks every language and never leaves your desk.

Baku is the Naga AI agent, built into every DIWA and developed with the Machine.Fun ecosystem. It takes its name from the Bakunawa, the sea serpent of Philippine myth, and like the Bakunawa it keeps its pearl close: your prompts, files and answers stay on the machine.

  • Private by design

    Everything is processed on DIWA. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you send it.

  • Works offline

    No connection needed. Baku runs on the 50 TOPS NPU and 64GB of memory inside the box.

  • Speaks every language

    Ask in English, Filipino, Spanish, Japanese or any other language. Baku answers in the one you use.

  • Plays well with others

    DIWA also runs Ollama, LM Studio and ComfyUI, so other models can sit beside Baku.
